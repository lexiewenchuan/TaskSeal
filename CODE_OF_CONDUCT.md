# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TaskSeal is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous
community. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- state uncertainty and implementation status honestly;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property;
- give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private
information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other public spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and
focused on keeping the community safe and productive.
